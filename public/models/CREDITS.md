## Marian sculpture

Virgin and Child / Bruges Madonna, after Michelangelo, plaster cast KAS225.
Digital source: Statens Museum for Kunst (SMK Open), Copenhagen.
Public domain / CC0 digital reproduction, as documented by SMK and Wikimedia Commons.

- https://api.smk.dk/api/v1/art?object_number=KAS225
- https://api.smk.dk/api/v1/download-3d/h128nk494_KAS225_small.stl
- https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Michelangelo_Buonarroti,_Maria_siddende_med_barnet,_Br%C3%BCggemadonna,_,_KAS225,_Statens_Museum_for_Kunst,_3D_model.stl
- https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Changes: converted STL to GLB; quadric decimation to 45,000 and 6,000 triangles; normalized and oriented in the viewer; new marble material. These models depict a historical Madonna and Child sculpture, **not a survey or scan of a statue at Phương Lạc**. Placement and shrine architecture are reconstructions.
